Privacy-law analysis suite

Personal project · ongoing

A suite of LLM agent workflows for privacy and AI compliance analysis: applicability triage across the GDPR, the EU AI Act, and the U.S. state privacy laws; rights-request handling; impact-assessment scoping; and breach-notification analysis. Every conclusion is checked against primary sources, with a currency ledger so nothing rests on a stale effective date.

#LLMAgents #GDPR #EUAIAct #StatePrivacyLaw

Autonomous paper-trading system

Personal project · ongoing

A multi-agent system that researches, sizes, and places simulated trades autonomously, with hard safety interlocks pinned by tests so it can never touch a live account. Built to explore how far agentic architectures can be trusted with real-world authority, and where guardrails have to be structural rather than behavioral.
